# Research Synthesis

- Start from the question the final synthesis must answer.
- Keep relevant claims from all encountered sources without filtering by perceived trustworthiness.
- Distinguish source claims, observations, and your own inference.
- Preserve contradictions, minority positions, uncertainty, and missing evidence.
- Organize findings by the user's decision or question rather than by search order.
- Finish with a readable synthesis, concrete conclusions where supported, and a complete source list.
- Avoid dumping raw notes when a structured explanation communicates the evidence better.

The adult user decides source relevance and credibility.
